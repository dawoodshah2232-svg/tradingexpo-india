Frequently Asked Questions

What does the two-day agenda look like?

Day 1 opens with keynotes and moves through panels, workshops and the exhibition floor, closing with networking sessions. Day 2 continues with more keynotes, deep-dive workshops, live trading demos and the awards night finale. Both days blend stage content with floor time so you never have to choose between learning and networking.

Who are the speakers at Trading Expo India 2027?

The conference features 80+ speakers — traders, brokers, fintech founders, analysts, educators and market experts from India and beyond. The full speaker lineup is announced in phases closer to the event. Follow the agenda page and announcements for reveals, session topics and the final two-day schedule.

Are workshops included in my ticket?

Workshop access depends on your pass. Pro Trader and VIP passes include the full workshop program across both days. Trader pass holders get exhibition floor and networking access; workshops can be added subject to availability. Check the tickets page for exactly what each pass includes before you book.

Will conference sessions be recorded?

Key sessions are planned to be recorded, with highlights shared after the event through official channels. Full session recordings and availability depend on the final program and speaker permissions. The best experience is live — the Q&A, networking and spontaneous floor conversations cannot be captured on video.

Is there an awards night? When is it?

Yes — the awards night is a flagship part of the program, celebrating outstanding performers across the trading and fintech ecosystem. It takes place on the evening of the expo program, bringing together speakers, sponsors, exhibitors and VIP guests. Award categories and the nomination process will be announced closer to the event.

Can I suggest a speaker or session topic?

Absolutely. The program team welcomes speaker suggestions and session ideas from the community — especially practitioners with real market experience. Reach out through the contact page with the speaker's background and proposed topic. The final program is curated to balance education, market insight and practical trading content.

What language are the sessions in?

Sessions are conducted primarily in English, the working language of India's trading and fintech community. If any sessions include other languages, it will be clearly marked in the final schedule. All stage signage, program materials and the event app experience are in English as standard.

When will the full detailed schedule be published?

The detailed hour-by-hour schedule is published closer to the event, once the full speaker lineup and session grid are confirmed. The agenda page already shows the two-day structure and session formats. Check back regularly or watch official announcements — ticket holders are notified as soon as the final schedule goes live.
