Frequently Asked Questions

How do I contact the Trading Expo India 2027 team?

Use the contact page form — choose the topic that matches your enquiry, such as exhibiting, sponsorship, speaking, tickets or media, and the right team will respond. You can also reach the partnerships and support teams through the official channels listed on the page. Expect a reply within one business day.

I want to exhibit — who should I talk to?

Start with the exhibit page reservation form, or choose the exhibiting topic on the contact page. The partnerships team will respond within one business day to discuss booth sizes, pricing, floor locations and timelines. They stay with you through the exhibitor portal from reservation all the way to event day.

How do I become a sponsor?

Visit the sponsors page to review the tiers — Title, Platinum, Gold, Silver and specialty packages — then contact the partnerships team through the contact page. They will understand your goals, recommend the right package and can customize branding, stage and awards-night options. Sponsorship inventory is limited, so start the conversation early.

How can I apply to speak at the conference?

The program team welcomes speaker applications from traders, brokers, fintech leaders, analysts and educators with real market experience. Send your background, proposed topic and why it matters to the audience through the contact page. The final 80+ speaker lineup is curated and announced in phases.

I am from the press — how do I get media access?

Media representatives can request press accreditation through the contact page by choosing the media topic. Share your publication, role and coverage plans, and the communications team will respond with accreditation details. Accredited media get access to the press area, speaker interviews by arrangement and official event assets.

How quickly will I get a reply?

The team aims to reply to every enquiry within one business day. Exhibiting and sponsorship queries go to the partnerships team, while ticket and general questions go to support. For urgent matters close to the event, use the priority contact details listed on the contact page and mark your message as urgent.

Can I book tickets for a group or company?

Yes — group and corporate passes are available at preferential rates for teams, trading communities and companies. Share your group size through the contact page and the team will arrange a tailored quote. Group bookings keep everyone under one reservation while each attendee still receives their own individual ticket.

Where do I report an issue with my booking or ticket?

Contact support through the contact page with your booking reference and a description of the issue — missing ticket email, name correction, transfer or upgrade request. The team resolves most booking issues within one business day. You can also log in to the ticket holder portal to re-download your ticket instantly.
