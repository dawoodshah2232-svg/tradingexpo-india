Frequently Asked Questions

Where exactly is Trading Expo India 2027 taking place?

The expo takes place in India on 23–24 April 2027. The host city and venue are being finalized and will be announced soon on the venue page and through official channels. Once announced, you will find full details here — address, how to reach it, nearby hotels and on-site facilities.

When will the venue be announced?

The host city and venue announcement is coming soon. The venue page is the first place it will appear, followed by announcements to ticket holders, exhibitors and followers. If you have already booked, you will be notified directly — so make sure the email on your booking is correct and check the portal for updates.

What are the event timings on both days?

The expo runs across two full days, 23–24 April 2027, with doors opening in the morning and programming continuing into the evening — including the awards night. Exact opening and closing times will be confirmed with the venue announcement. Ticket holders receive the final timings by email and in the portal before the event.

How do I reach the venue?

Once the host city and venue are announced, this page will carry complete travel guidance — nearest airport, railway and metro connectivity, cab and parking options, and estimated travel times. The venue is being chosen for easy access for both domestic and international visitors. Check back after the official announcement.

Where should I stay during the expo?

Partner hotel recommendations near the venue will be published once the host city is confirmed — including options across budgets and group booking assistance. Staying near the venue means easy access to both event days and the awards night. Watch the venue page and announcements; early hotel booking is advised once details are live.

Is the venue accessible for people with disabilities?

Yes — accessibility is a priority in the venue selection. The final venue will offer step-free access, accessible restrooms and seating accommodations. If you have specific accessibility needs, contact the team in advance through the contact page and they will coordinate with the venue to make your visit comfortable.

Is parking available at the venue?

Parking details will be confirmed with the venue announcement — including on-site parking capacity, nearby alternatives and guidance for exhibitors with booth materials. The venue is being selected with strong transport links so metro, cabs and shuttles are easy options too. Full logistics will be published on this page.

What should I bring to the expo?

Bring your ticket (digital is fine), a valid photo ID, business cards and a charged phone — the portal, networking and session updates all run through it. If you are exhibiting, your booth materials and team badges are managed through the exhibitor portal. Comfortable shoes help: the exhibition floor is big and you will walk a lot.
