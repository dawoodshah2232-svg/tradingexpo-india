About the event

Where Traders, Brokers
and Technology meet.

Trading Expo India is a two-day ticketed exhibition and conference created for India's rapidly expanding community of traders, investors, financial professionals and fintech businesses.

The event connects the B2C trading community with the B2B financial ecosystem under one roof. Visitors discover trading platforms, financial products, market technologies, analytics tools and education providers — while meeting the companies and professionals behind them.

For participating companies, Trading Expo India provides direct access to thousands of traders, investors, affiliates, introducing brokers, financial professionals, entrepreneurs and potential business partners.

Trading Expo is not simply an exhibition.
It is where the future of trading is built.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

When is Trading Expo India 2027?

Trading Expo India will take place on 23 and 24 April 2027.

Is the event free?

No. Trading Expo India is a ticketed event with passes starting from ₹249 during the Early Bird period.

Can one ticket cover both days?

Yes. Unless otherwise specified during a special promotion, event passes provide access according to the two-day benefits stated for the selected ticket category.

Who can attend?

The event is intended for traders, investors, financial professionals, fintech professionals, entrepreneurs, students and people interested in financial markets and trading technology, subject to the event's admission policy.

Can companies exhibit?

Yes. Exhibition booths and sponsorship packages are available to approved companies.

How can I become a speaker?

Complete the Speaker Application form. Applications will be reviewed by the conference team.

Can influencers partner with Trading Expo?

Yes. Trading and finance creators can apply to participate in our community and referral partner programme.

Are tickets refundable?

Tickets are generally non-refundable except where required under applicable law or where otherwise specified in the event's official terms and conditions.
