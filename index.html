FAQ

Questions, answered.

When is Trading Expo India 2027?

23 and 24 April 2027, in India. The venue city will be announced soon.

How much are tickets?

Early bird passes start at ₹249 (Trader), ₹999 (Pro Trader) and ₹2,499 (VIP). Prices rise as the event approaches.

Can my company exhibit?

Yes — booths and sponsorships are available to approved companies. Reserve your space on the Exhibit page.

Is there an exhibitor portal?

Yes. After reserving, log in to the Exhibitor Portal with your booking reference to manage tickets, team badges and branding uploads.

Are tickets refundable?

Tickets are generally non-refundable except where required by law or stated in the official terms.
