General

When and where is Trading Expo India 2027?

23–24 April 2027, in India. The venue city will be announced soon — ask to be notified.

Who organises the event?

Trading Expo India is organised by ProFX Media FZ-LLC.

Who should attend?

Retail and professional traders, investors, brokers, fintech companies, IBs, affiliates, educators, media and anyone interested in financial markets.

Tickets

How much are tickets?

Early bird: Trader Pass ₹249 (regular ₹499), Pro Trader Pass ₹999 (regular ₹1,499), VIP Pass ₹2,499 (regular ₹3,999). Book now →

What's the difference between the passes?

Trader covers the 2-day exhibition. Pro Trader adds full conference access, priority seating and fast-track entry. VIP adds the VIP lounge, reserved seating and exclusive networking.

How do I receive my ticket?

Your ticket is emailed to you automatically after booking, and you can download or print it anytime from the portal using your booking reference and password.

Are tickets refundable?

Tickets are generally non-refundable except where required by law or stated in the official terms. If the event is cancelled, ticket holders will be refunded.

Is there a lucky draw?

Yes — every ticket is automatically entered into the Trading Expo lucky draw held on Day 2 (24 April).

Do you offer group tickets?

Yes — special packages for trading communities, colleges, corporate teams and affiliate networks. Enquire here →
