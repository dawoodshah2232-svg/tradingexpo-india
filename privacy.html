Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 October 2026

Trading Expo India 2027 (“the Expo”, “we”) is organised by ProFX Media FZ-LLC. This policy explains what personal information we collect when you use this website, and how we use it.

Information we collect

  • Ticket bookings: your name, email address and phone number, plus the ticket type you reserve, so we can confirm your booking and send event updates.
  • Contact enquiries: the details you send us (name, email, message) when you ask about tickets, exhibiting, sponsorships, speaking or press.
  • Newsletter: your email address if you subscribe to event updates.
  • Portal accounts: booking reference and credentials you create to manage tickets and announcements.
  • Preferences stored on your device: your light/dark theme choice is saved in your browser’s local storage and never leaves your device.

We do not collect payment details on this website. No payment is taken at the time of reservation; our team follows up with a payment link separately.

How we use your information

  • To process and confirm ticket reservations and respond to enquiries.
  • To send event updates you asked for (agenda, venue, speaker announcements). You can opt out at any time.
  • To operate the attendee/exhibitor portal and keep it secure.
  • To improve the website and understand aggregate, non-identifying usage.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only where necessary to run the event — for example with our email provider to send confirmations, or where required by law.

Data retention

We keep booking and enquiry records for as long as needed for the event and any follow-up, then delete or anonymise them. You may ask us to delete your data sooner (see below).

Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of the personal data we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Write to info@tradingexpo.com and we will respond within a reasonable time.

Children

This event and website are intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the event’s systems evolve (for example when online payments go live). The latest version will always be posted on this page.

Contact

Questions about privacy: info@tradingexpo.com — ProFX Media FZ-LLC, organiser of Trading Expo India 2027, 23–24 April 2027, India.
