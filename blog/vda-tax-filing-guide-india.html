Filing Crypto Taxes in India: A Practical VDA Reporting Guide

Trading Expo India editorial team·September 24, 2026·3 min read

← Back to all articles

Knowing the 30% rule is step one; reporting it correctly on your income tax return is step two — and step two is where most traders stumble. India's ITR forms now have dedicated schedules for virtual digital assets, and the tax department cross-references exchange-reported data.

This practical guide covers the reporting workflow. It is not tax advice — for that, hire a CA who understands VDAs.

The VDA schedule: where crypto goes in your ITR

Gains from VDAs are reported in the dedicated VDA schedule of the ITR form — separate from capital gains schedules for shares or property. You report each transfer's consideration, cost of acquisition and resulting gain; intraday-style high-volume traders may need aggregated reporting formats their CA recommends.

Choosing the right base ITR form matters too: the form must support the VDA schedule and your other income sources. Filing on the wrong form and omitting the schedule is one of the commonest errors.

Documenting swaps, staking and airdrops

Crypto-to-crypto swaps count as transfers — each one needs date, pair, quantities and rupee fair-market value at the time. Staking rewards, mining income and airdrops each have their own characterisation questions that remain genuinely debated among professionals; this is exactly where a knowledgeable CA earns their fee.

Export your complete transaction history from every exchange and wallet before filing season. APIs break, exchanges shut down, and CSV exports have a habit of disappearing when you need them most.

Reconciling TDS with Form 26AS/AIS

The 1% TDS deducted on your sales appears in your Form 26AS and Annual Information Statement. Reconcile these against your own records before filing — mismatches between exchange-reported figures and your return are a classic notice trigger.

If TDS was deducted but does not appear in your statements, chase the deductor early. And remember: TDS is not your final tax — it is advance tax adjusted against your 30% liability.

Gifts, transfers and edge cases the guides skip

Standard guides cover buys and sells; real portfolios are messier. Crypto received as gifts carries its own tax questions — valuation at receipt, the giver's documentation, and how subsequent gains are computed — that generic advice glosses over. Wallet-to-wallet transfers between your own wallets are not disposals, but you must be able to prove both wallets are yours if questioned, which means maintaining address records. Staking rewards, liquidity-pool income, airdrops and mining rewards each sit in genuinely debated territory among professionals: is receipt taxable, or only disposal? Honest answer: interpretations differ, and this is precisely where paying for a knowledgeable CA outperforms free internet opinions.

Cross-exchange arbitrage traders face multiplied complexity — hundreds of transfers across platforms, each potentially a TDS event, with cost-basis tracking across venues. If your activity is high-volume, professional portfolio-tracking software is not optional; it is the only reliable way to compute liabilities. And for anyone with DeFi activity, export on-chain history regularly: blockchain data persists, but your ability to interpret years-old transactions from memory does not. Edge cases are where assessments are won or lost — document them as carefully as your straightforward trades.

Deadlines, notices and professional help

File by the applicable due date, pay advance tax on schedule if your liability is significant, and respond to any notices promptly with documentation — silence converts enquiries into assessments. A CA experienced in VDA taxation typically pays for themselves in avoided penalties alone.

Frequently asked questions

Which ITR form do I use for crypto gains?

Crypto gains go in the dedicated VDA schedule. The base ITR form depends on your overall income profile and must support that schedule — confirm with your CA rather than guessing.

How do I value crypto-to-crypto swaps in rupees?

Each swap needs the rupee fair-market value at the time of the transaction for both legs. Portfolio-tracking tools can compute this automatically from historical price data.

What if my exchange didn't deduct TDS?

On P2P or non-compliant platforms the TDS obligation may fall on you as buyer. Unresolved TDS gaps surface in AIS mismatches — address them before filing, with professional help.
