What to expect at Trading Expo India 2027.

Trading Expo India editorial team·September 25, 2026·7 min read

← Back to all articles

On 23–24 April 2027, India hosts its most ambitious gathering for the online trading community. Whether you're a first-time visitor or a seasoned expo-goer, here's an honest walkthrough of what your two days will look like — so you can plan, pack and make every hour count.

The exhibition floor: the heart of the event

The floor is where the expo lives. Across two days, 70–80 exhibitors — brokers, trading platforms, fintech companies, technology providers, educators and media — will run live booths with product demonstrations, one-on-one consultations and exclusive event offers.

Unlike scrolling through a comparison site, the floor lets you touch the products: open a demo account with a broker's team beside you, watch an analytics platform crunch live data, or compare execution speeds across providers in real time. Bring your questions — the people who built the products will be standing right there.

  • Brokers & platforms: compare trading conditions, account types and onboarding face to face.
  • Trading technology: charting tools, algos, AI assistants, APIs and automation — demonstrated live.
  • Fintech & services: payments, CRM systems, compliance tech and market infrastructure.
  • Education & media: meet the educators and creators whose content you've been following.

The conference: 80+ voices across two days

Running alongside the exhibition, the conference programme features 80+ speakers — traders, analysts, founders, CEOs, technology leaders and educators. Expect keynotes on the future of online trading, panels on AI and algorithmic trading, fireside chats on trading psychology and risk management, and sessions on fintech innovation and building a trading business.

The draft agenda runs from the opening ceremony on the morning of Day 1 through the closing ceremony on Day 2, with networking breaks built in so you never have to choose between a great session and a great conversation. Conference access depends on your pass type — Pro Trader and VIP passes include full conference access.

Come for the products, stay for the people: the conversations between sessions are where expos quietly pay for themselves.

Networking, engineered in

With 10,000+ expected visitors, Trading Expo India is a networking opportunity on a national scale. Dedicated networking areas, a networking lounge and structured breaks mean you're never more than a coffee away from your next useful contact — whether that's a mentor, a business partner, an affiliate connection or simply a trader who thinks like you do.

Tip: bring business cards or a digital card on your phone, and decide in advance who you want to meet — exhibitors you shortlist beforehand get far more value from the floor than those who wander.

Live demos and product launches

Expos are where companies choose to show off. Expect live platform walkthroughs, hands-on technology zones and product announcements timed for the event audience. If you're evaluating a new broker or tool, there is no better due diligence than watching it work live and interrogating the team behind it.

The lucky draw

Every ticket is automatically an entry into the Trading Expo lucky draw, held on Day 2. It's our way of saying thanks for being part of the first edition — and one more reason to be in the room when it happens.

Planning your visit

The venue city is still to be announced — watch the venue page for updates as it's confirmed. Doors open in the morning on both days; the full timed schedule is on the agenda page. Passes start at ₹249 early bird for the Trader Pass, with Pro Trader (₹999) and VIP (₹2,499) options for the full experience.

Group packages are available for trading communities, colleges and corporate teams — enquire through the contact page if you're bringing a crowd.

Ready to experience it?

Early-bird pricing is live now and allocations are limited. Lock in your pass for 23–24 April 2027.
