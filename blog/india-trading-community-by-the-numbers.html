The trends behind the numbers

Figures only matter with context. Here's what we're seeing in India's trading community — described as it is, without inflated claims:

  • Mobile-first participation. A generation that banks, shops and learns on smartphones is bringing the same expectations to trading — pushing brokers and platforms to design for the phone first.
  • Payments trust. Years of everyday digital payments have made Indians comfortable moving money online, lowering the psychological barrier to funding trading accounts.
  • Content-driven discovery. Trading educators, finance creators and online communities have made market participation a mainstream conversation — the expo floor is where that conversation becomes face to face.
  • Homegrown fintech. Indian engineering teams are building trading technology used well beyond India's borders, adding a B2B dimension to what looks from outside like a retail story.
  • Community infrastructure. Trading communities, academies and affiliate networks are professionalising fast — exactly the groups our group ticket packages are designed for.
