How to Verify a Broker's SEBI Registration in 5 Minutes

Trading Expo India editorial team·September 24, 2026·3 min read

← Back to all articles

Every scam broker has one thing in common: they are counting on you not to check. Verifying a broker's SEBI registration is the fastest, highest-value due-diligence step in Indian trading — and it takes less time than brewing tea.

Here is the exact process, plus the clone-website traps that catch even careful traders.

Step 1: Find the registration number

Legitimate brokers display their SEBI registration number on their website — usually in the footer or on a compliance page — along with their registered office address and the segments they serve. Copy that number exactly. If a broker hides it, buries it, or gets evasive when asked, stop there.

Treat absence of a registration number the way you would treat a restaurant with no kitchen: whatever they are offering, it is not what you came for.

Step 2: Cross-check on SEBI's database

Go to SEBI's official website and search its intermediary/member database for the registration number and the broker's exact legal name. Confirm three things: the registration is active, the name matches letter-for-letter, and the authorised segments include the markets you want to trade (equity, currency derivatives, commodity).

Do this by typing sebi.gov.in into your browser yourself. Links from advertisements, social media or 'account managers' can lead to convincing fakes of the regulator's own site.

Step 3: Defeat the clone websites

Clone scams copy a real broker's name, logo and even registration number onto a lookalike domain. The defences: check the domain character by character (rnidfc vs ruidfc tricks are common), verify contact details against the exchange's official member list, and never deposit through links sent over WhatsApp or Telegram.

One more powerful test: call the broker's publicly listed phone number and ask them to confirm the website domain you are looking at. A two-minute call defeats most clones.

Exchange membership and SCORES: the deeper verification layer

SEBI registration is the first check; exchange membership is the second. A broker must be a member of each exchange where it offers you products — NSE, BSE, MCX memberships are granted separately — so confirm membership on the exchange's official member list, not just the broker's claims. While there, check for any disciplinary actions the exchange has published against the member: fines, suspensions or warnings are public record and reveal patterns that a clean-looking website hides. This two-source verification (regulator plus exchange) closes most gaps that scammers exploit.

The third layer is SEBI's SCORES platform — the official grievance redressal system. Before committing significant capital, search for the broker's complaint record: volume of complaints and, more importantly, resolution speed and pendency. A broker drowning in unresolved complaints is telling you about its operational culture. Finally, verify the fund trail: your money should move only between your bank account and the broker's designated client account, with contract notes issued for every trade. Any request to transfer funds to a personal account, a different company name, or via crypto is an immediate walk-away signal, no matter how convincing the explanation.

Frequently asked questions

Where do I find a broker's SEBI registration number?

On the broker's website footer or compliance page. Then verify it independently in SEBI's official intermediary database — never trust the number on the broker's site alone.

Can scammers fake a SEBI registration number?

Yes — they copy real numbers onto clone websites. Always cross-check the domain, confirm details via the exchange's member list, and call the broker's public phone number to confirm the website.

What if a broker is registered abroad but not with SEBI?

Foreign registration does not authorise a broker to serve Indian residents in regulated segments, nor does it give you access to SEBI's grievance redressal. For Indian market access, SEBI registration is what counts.
