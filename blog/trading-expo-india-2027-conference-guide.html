Trading Expo India 2027 Conference Guide: Keynotes, Panels & What to Attend

With 80+ speakers across two days, the Trading Expo India 2027 conference is where the industry's thinking gets aired: keynotes on the big picture, panels debating contested questions, and workshops going deep on craft. But abundance creates a problem — you cannot attend everything.

This guide helps you plan your two days (23–24 April 2027) for maximum value.

The session formats, decoded

Keynotes deliver the big-picture narratives — where markets, regulation and technology are heading — from the industry's most experienced voices. Panels stage structured disagreement on contested topics (regulation, crypto's future, active vs systematic trading), which is where the most honest insights surface. Workshops and demos go hands-on: platforms, strategies and tools explained in working detail.

A balanced schedule mixes all three: keynotes for direction, panels for debate, workshops for skill.

Plan by objective, not by FOMO

Before the agenda is published, define your objectives: learning a strategy, choosing technology, understanding regulation, building network. Then allocate sessions to objectives — and accept that you will miss good sessions. A plan you follow beats a perfect plan you abandon.

Build in floor time between sessions: back-to-back keynotes with no booth visits wastes the exhibition half of the expo.

How to extract value from sessions

Sit near the front, take notes by hand, and write down one action per session — a concept to research, a tool to trial, a person to contact. Ask questions: speakers remember engaged attendees, and your question often helps dozens of silent listeners. Skip sessions that do not serve your objectives, even popular ones.

The real conference happens twice: once in the hall, once in your notes afterwards. Protect time for the second.

Making the most of Q&A: asking questions speakers remember

Conference Q&A sessions are underused leverage: a good question extracts personalised insight from an expert, signals your seriousness to everyone listening, and often starts conversations that continue into networking breaks. The formula for a memorable question: brief context ('I swing-trade Nifty options with 1% risk per trade'), a specific tension ('I find my winners reverse before reaching 2R in low-volatility regimes'), and a precise ask ('do you adjust targets by volatility regime, and if so, how?'). Contrast this with the questions speakers dread — long autobiographies, requests for stock tips, or challenges disguised as questions. Specificity respects the speaker's time and earns a substantive answer.

Prepare two or three questions per must-attend session in advance, and sit where microphones reach easily. Listen to other attendees' questions too — they reveal what the room is struggling with and often surface angles you missed. If your question is too specific for the public session, use it as your opener when approaching the speaker afterwards: 'I had a question about adjusting targets by volatility regime…' transitions naturally into the one-on-one conversations where the deepest learning happens. Speakers remember engaged questioners; be one, and the conference keeps teaching after the microphones switch off.
